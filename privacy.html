Privacy Policy

Clashh Qualified Roofing Pros ("we", "us", "our")
Effective date: October 1, 2026

1. Who we are

We operate a website that lets property owners request roofing help and connects them with a roofing contractor. We are not a roofing contractor. Contact us at sclashh@yahoo.com. Mailing address: 7509 NW 52nd St, #SPD-9303, Miami, FL 33166.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a request we collect the information you enter:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Property street address, unit or apartment number, city, state and ZIP code
  • Whether you own the property
  • What your roof needs, approximate property size, a description of the problem, how soon you want the work done, and how you plan to pay (for example insurance, cash or financing)

We may also collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the date and time of your request, and information about the ad or link that brought you to our site.

3. How we use your information

  • To review and verify your request
  • To match your request with a roofing contractor and provide your information to that contractor
  • To prevent fraud, spam and invalid requests
  • To operate, measure and improve our service and our advertising

4. Verification services

To check that requests are real, we send parts of your information to service providers that verify it: a mapping and address-validation provider (Google) for your address, a phone-verification provider (Twilio) for your phone number, and an email-verification provider (ZeroBounce) for your email address. These providers process the data to give us a result and are subject to their own privacy terms.

5. Who we share your information with

  • One roofing contractor. With your consent, we share your request and contact details with one (1) roofing contractor, who may contact you by phone, text message (including automated messages) and email about your project. Before that, we may show candidate contractors a summary that does not identify you (for example ZIP code, property size and type of work).
  • Service providers that host our website, process form submissions, send email and perform the verifications described above.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law or to protect rights, safety and against fraud.

The contractor pays us for the request. Under some state laws this may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out (see section 8). We do not sell your information to anyone other than the contractor described above.

6. Calls and text messages

By submitting the form you agree that the contractor may contact you by phone and text, including with automated technology, at the number you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchasing anything. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to a text to opt out, and you can ask the contractor or us to stop calling.

7. Advertising

We advertise on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram (Meta). If you arrive from an ad, the platform may collect information about your visit under its own privacy policy. If we install advertising tools on our site in the future, we will update this policy.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop sharing it with contractors. Residents of certain states, including California, have additional rights under state privacy laws, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. To make a request, email sclashh@yahoo.com with the subject "Privacy Request" and the name, phone number and email you used on the form. We may need to verify your identity.

9. Retention

We keep your information for 12 months after your request, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We may keep limited records longer only where needed to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. A roofing contractor who receives your information keeps it under its own policies.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Children

Our service is for property owners and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
